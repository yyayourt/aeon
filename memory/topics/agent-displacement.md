# Agent Displacement Tracker

*Last run: 2026-06-04*

## Known Displacement Events (baseline + updates)
- Klarna (2024): replaced 700 customer support agents with AI. Resolution time 2min vs 11min human avg. By 2026, headcount fell from 7,400 to ~3,000; quietly rebuilding some human support capacity.
- Duolingo (2024): cut ~10% of contractors, cited AI content generation replacing human translators.
- Salesforce (2025–2026): reduced customer support function from 9,000 to ~5,000, citing AI agents. CEO Marc Benioff cited AI agent handle rate; company redeployed some to other roles.
- IBM (2024): eliminated ~8,000 positions ($640M annual savings), paused hiring on ~7,800 back-office roles AI could replace.
- Cloudflare (May 8, 2026): 1,100 roles cut (20% of company) — first major layoff in 16-year history. Support staff explicitly targeted. AI usage up 600% in 3 months; employees report 2–100x productivity. Record Q1 revenue ($639.8M, +34% YoY).
- Intuit (May 20, 2026): 3,000 employees (17%) laid off. TurboTax/QuickBooks maker redirecting to AI product development. Workforce: 18,200 → ~15,200.
- ClickUp (May 25, 2026): 22% of workforce cut; CEO cited 3,000 internal AI agents handling complex tasks. "AI-first" framing; introducing outsized pay bands for AI-leveraged performers.
- GM (May 11, 2026): ~600 salaried IT workers laid off (>10% of IT dept). Deliberate skills swap — clearing traditional IT, hiring AI-native: prompt engineers, model/agent developers, data engineers.
